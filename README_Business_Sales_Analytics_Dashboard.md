# 📊 Business Sales Analytics Dashboard

> An end-to-end business data analytics project using Excel, Power Query, Pivot Tables, Power BI, and DAX to transform sales data into clear business insights.

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Business Sales Analytics Dashboard** is a practical data analytics project focused on an Electric & Electronics business.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end workflow: preparing raw transaction data, cleaning and transforming it, analyzing business performance, and presenting the results through interactive dashboards and KPI reporting.

The goal is to help management understand sales performance across regions, products, and customer types while monitoring key business metrics such as revenue and cost.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Compare performance across regions
- Identify high-performing products and categories
- Compare Corporate, Retail, and Wholesale customers
- Analyze revenue and cost
- Identify important business trends
- Present KPIs through an interactive dashboard
- Support data-driven business decisions
